Portal World Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Portal World ("the app") is published by DevDreamAi. This policy explains what data the app uses.

What we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email, contacts, location or any account. Your game progress and best scores are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model and app interactions to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and some US states), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice anytime from the Privacy options button in the app. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party software

The app is built with open-source software listed in its credits (three.js, Capacitor and others). These libraries do not collect data on their own.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes on this page. Questions: dev@conglomerate-ai.com.
